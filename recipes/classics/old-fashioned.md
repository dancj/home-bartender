## Notes

Swap the sweetener to change the drink without changing the build: demerara, maple, or agave (with reposado + mezcal it becomes the Oaxaca Old Fashioned).

Smoke it under a cloche for 10–20 seconds, or use fat-washed bourbon — see the Maple Smoked and Maple Bacon Old Fashioneds.
