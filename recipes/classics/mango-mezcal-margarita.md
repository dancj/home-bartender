## Notes

Reducing store-bought mango nectar by half intensifies the fruit without watering down the spirit. Dilution water is built into the batch to account for the water that shaking with ice would otherwise add. Super lime juice is a shelf-stable acid-adjusted lime substitute. Source: GitHub issue #193.
