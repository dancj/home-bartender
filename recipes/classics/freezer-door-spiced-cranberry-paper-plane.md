## Notes

The water stands in for the dilution you'd get from shaking with ice. With citrus, water, and syrup in the bottle it can turn slushy or partly freeze; if so, pull it 30 minutes before serving and shake well. Because of the fresh lemon juice, drink it within 1–2 days.

Swaps from the source: Campari for Aperol, rye for bourbon, or orange or grapefruit juice for lemon. Source: [Join Jules](https://joinjules.com/recipes/freezer-door-spiced-cranberry-paper-plane/).
