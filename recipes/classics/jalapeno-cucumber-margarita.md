## Notes

Infuse the spirit directly rather than adding fresh cucumber or jalapeño juice to a batch — it prevents oxidation and off-flavors over the course of a party. Dilution water is built into the batch to account for the water that shaking with ice would otherwise add. Super lime juice is a shelf-stable acid-adjusted lime substitute. Source: GitHub issue #193.
