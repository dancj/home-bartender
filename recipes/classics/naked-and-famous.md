## Notes

Created by Joaquín Simó at Death & Co, NYC. An equal-parts cocktail — the easiest format to remember and scale. Yellow Chartreuse is essential; Green Chartreuse is too herbal and overpowers the balance. The smokiness of mezcal is what makes this more interesting than its cousin, the Paper Plane.

**Yellow Chartreuse substitute:** Yellow Chartreuse can be hard to find and expensive. Strega — a saffron-forward Italian herbal liqueur — works well as a substitute. It's similarly sweet and herbal with a warm golden character that holds up in the equal-parts format.
