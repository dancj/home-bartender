## Notes

Created by Sam Ross at Milk & Honey, NYC. The equal-parts template at its most elegant — everything in balance, nothing dominating. Amaro Nonino is essential; other amaros will change the character significantly. The card lists two variations:

**Variations:**
1. Sub mezcal for bourbon — adds smoke, keeps the bitter/citrus balance
2. **"Naked and Famous"** — sub mezcal for bourbon *and* Yellow Chartreuse for Amaro Nonino; this is essentially the Naked & Famous recipe
