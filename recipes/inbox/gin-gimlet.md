## Ingredients

- 2 oz gin
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice
- ¾ oz simple syrup

**Variation:** Add 1–2 oz sparkling water, serve in a Collins glass over ice for a longer, more refreshing build.

---

## Steps

1. Combine gin, lime juice, and simple syrup in a shaker with ice
2. Shake well until chilled
3. Strain into a chilled coupe

*For the Collins variation:* Shake as above, strain into a Collins glass over ice, top with sparkling water and stir gently.

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings:*

- 16 oz gin
- 6 oz fresh lime juice
- 6 oz simple syrup

Combine and refrigerate. Shake each serving individually with ice and strain.

---

## Notes

One of the simplest and most reliable gin drinks. The key is fresh lime — Rose's sweetened lime cordial is traditional but fresh juice with simple syrup is crisper. A floral or contemporary gin (like Hendrick's) works well; a classic London Dry (like Tanqueray) keeps it clean and bright.
