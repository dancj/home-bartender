## Ingredients

- 1 oz limoncello
- ½ oz Aperol
- ¼ oz simple syrup
- ¼ oz fresh lemon juice
- 2 oz Prosecco
- ½ oz seltzer

---

## Steps

1. Combine limoncello, Aperol, simple syrup, and lemon juice in a shaker with ice
2. Shake briefly until chilled
3. Strain into a wine glass over ice
4. Top with Prosecco and seltzer, stir gently

---

## How to Batch It

*My 6x batch from the card:*

- 6 oz limoncello
- 3 oz Aperol
- 3 oz simple syrup
- 1½ oz fresh lemon juice

Combine and refrigerate. Pour ~2 oz of mix per serving over ice, top with Prosecco and a splash of seltzer to order.

---

## Notes

A more composed, cocktail-forward take on the Aperol Spritz — the limoncello and fresh lemon juice add real citrus depth that the classic recipe lacks. The shaken-then-built method integrates the base flavors before adding the bubbles. I scaled this to 6x on the card — it gets made in quantity.
