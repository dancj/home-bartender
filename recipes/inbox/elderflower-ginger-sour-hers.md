# Elderflower Ginger Sour (Hers)


> Submitter suggested category: **classics**

## Notes

**Why this works:** St Germain's floral elderflower softens the ginger heat beautifully — it's approachable but still interesting and complex.

**Ginger gum syrup:** The silky texture from the gum arabic makes this drink feel luxurious. The heat level depends on how strong your syrup is — start at 0.5 oz.

**Super lime juice swap:** If you have your super lime juice ready, use it instead of lemon for a more aromatic citrus backbone.

**Vodka variation:** Swap tequila for vodka for an even cleaner, more floral version that lets the ginger and St Germain really shine.

**Add bubbles:** Top with 2 oz prosecco or sparkling water after straining for a lighter spritz version.
