# Bubbly Mojito
> *A rum mojito finished with sparkling wine — fresh, minty, and celebratory.*

**Glass:** Collins or highball
**Method:** Shaken + built
**Ice:** Crushed
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Flavors:** `HERBAL` `CITRUS` `FRUITY` `BUBBLY`

---

## Ingredients

- 6 fresh mint leaves
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice (½ lime, cut up and muddled)
- ½ oz simple syrup
- 2 oz white rum
- 3 oz sparkling wine (Prosecco or Champagne)

---

## Steps

1. In a shaker, muddle mint leaves, lime wedges, simple syrup, and rum together
2. Add crushed ice and shake briefly
3. Pour everything (including ice) into a Collins glass
4. Top with sparkling wine and stir gently to combine

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings:*

- ~48 fresh mint leaves
- 6 oz fresh lime juice + 4 limes quartered for muddling
- 4 oz simple syrup
- 16 oz white rum
- 24 oz sparkling wine (1 bottle)

Muddle mint and lime in batches, combine with rum and syrup, refrigerate. Pour over crushed ice to serve, top each glass with ~3 oz sparkling wine.

## Notes

📝 *This card may be a revised version of the Prosecco Mojito already in the book (`recipes/originals/prosecco-mojito.md`). Review and merge if appropriate — the key differences here are explicit simple syrup (½ oz) and the muddling method (mint + lime wedges together with the rum and syrup before shaking).*

The crushed ice + pour-everything-out technique keeps the drink integrated and well-diluted. Sparkling wine adds elegance; a light Prosecco works best to avoid overpowering the mint.
