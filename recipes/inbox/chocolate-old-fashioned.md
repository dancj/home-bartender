# Chocolate Old Fashioned
> *A rich, dessert-leaning Old Fashioned — bourbon deepened with chocolate liqueur and a whisper of maple.*

**Glass:** Rocks
**Method:** Stirred
**Ice:** Large cube
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Flavors:** `CHOCOLATE` `RICH` `SWEET` `SPIRIT-FORWARD`

---

## Ingredients

- 2 oz bourbon
- ½ oz cioccolato liqueur (chocolate liqueur — e.g. Tempus Fugit Crème de Cacao, Mozart, or Godiva)
- 1 bar spoon maple syrup *(card crossed out ¼ oz — use a spoon, not a full pour)*
- 2 dashes chocolate bitters (e.g. Bittermens Xocolatl Mole or Fee Brothers Aztec Chocolate)

**Garnish:** Orange peel (expressed)

---

## Steps

1. Combine bourbon, cioccolato liqueur, maple syrup, and bitters in a mixing glass with ice
2. Stir well until chilled and diluted (~30 seconds)
3. Strain over a large cube in a rocks glass
4. Express an orange peel over the glass, run around the rim, and drop in

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings:*

- 16 oz bourbon
- 4 oz cioccolato liqueur
- 8 bar spoons maple syrup (~2 oz)
- 16 dashes chocolate bitters

Combine and refrigerate. Add 2 oz water to pre-dilute. Stir briefly before serving over large cubes. Garnish to order.

---

## Notes

An original riff on the Old Fashioned. The key is restraint — maple syrup is just 1 bar spoon (the card crossed out ¼ oz as too much). The chocolate liqueur and bitters carry the chocolate character; the maple adds depth without making it sweet-heavy. Orange peel is essential for brightness against all that richness.
