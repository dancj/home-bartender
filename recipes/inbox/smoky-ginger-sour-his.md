# Smoky Ginger Sour (His)


> Submitter suggested category: **classics**

## Notes

**Why this works:** Mezcal's smoke, vermouth's herbal sweetness, Aperol's bitter orange and ginger's heat all layer beautifully with lemon tying it together.

**Vermouth:** Just enough to add complexity and round out the mezcal without making it sweet.

**Ginger gum syrup:** Homemade syrup adds silky texture AND heat — start at 0.5 oz and adjust up for more ginger punch.

**Scotch variation:** Swap mezcal for a peaty scotch for a completely different but equally interesting drink — smoky, herbal and gingery.
