# Habanero Mango Margarita

> *A sweet, spicy, citrusy margarita with house-made mango habanero syrup.*

---

## Ingredients

- 2 oz tequila blanco
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- ¾ oz mango habanero syrup
- ½ oz triple sec or Cointreau
- Tajin or salt rim

---

## House-Made Mango Habanero Syrup

*Makes ~3 oz. Keeps 1–2 weeks refrigerated.*

- ½ cup agave nectar
- ½ cup mango juice
- 2 habanero peppers, halved and de-stemmed
- Pinch of salt

1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan
2. Simmer for 5 minutes
3. Remove from heat and let sit for 20 minutes
4. Strain into a jar and refrigerate

---

## Steps

1. Rim glass with lime and tajin (or salt)
2. Combine tequila, lime juice, mango habanero syrup, and triple sec in a shaker with ice
3. Shake well until chilled
4. Strain into glass over ice

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings:*

- 16 oz tequila blanco
- 8 oz fresh lime juice
- 6 oz mango habanero syrup
- 4 oz triple sec

Combine and refrigerate. Shake individual portions or pour over ice. Rim glasses individually.

---

## Notes

Adjust heat by varying habanero steep time. For less spice, remove peppers after 10 minutes. For more, leave the full 20. Blanco tequila keeps the mango front and center — reposado will add earthiness if you prefer.
