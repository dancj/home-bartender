# Coconut Strawberry Tequila

> *A fruity, tropical tequila cocktail with a hint of smoke and heat — born at a house party.*

**Glass:** Rocks or coupe
**Method:** Shaken
**Ice:** Cubed or large cube
**Difficulty:** Easy

---

## Ingredients

- 1½ oz coconut tequila
- ½ oz mezcal
- ½ oz fresh lime juice
- ½ oz triple sec
- ½ tsp agave nectar
- Dash of hot bitters

---

## Steps

1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice
2. Shake well until chilled
3. Strain into a glass over ice

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings:*

- 12 oz coconut tequila
- 4 oz mezcal
- 4 oz fresh lime juice
- 4 oz triple sec
- 4 tsp agave nectar
- Hot bitters to taste

Combine and refrigerate. Shake individual portions with ice or serve over ice directly.

---

## Notes

Recipe by Wilson, developed 11/29. The mezcal adds backbone to what could otherwise be a sweet drink. Hot bitters are the secret weapon — just a dash ties everything together. The batched version (1 cup coconut tequila, ½ cup strawberry simple, ½ cup lemon juice) was the original — this single-serve is the refined winner.
