## Notes

Recipe by Wilson, developed 11/29. The mezcal adds backbone to what could otherwise be a sweet drink. Hot bitters are the secret weapon — just a dash ties everything together. The batched version (1 cup coconut tequila, ½ cup strawberry simple, ½ cup lemon juice) was the original — this single-serve is the refined winner.
