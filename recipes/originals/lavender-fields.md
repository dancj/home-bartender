## Ingredients

- 2 oz gin
- ½ oz elderflower liqueur (St-Germain)
- ¾ oz lavender honey syrup
- 1 egg white
- 2 dashes orange bitters

---

## House-Made Lavender Honey Syrup

*Makes ~6 oz. Keeps 2 weeks refrigerated.*

- 1½ oz dried lavender
- ½ cup honey
- ½ cup water

1. Combine honey and water in a saucepan and bring to a boil
2. Add lavender, reduce heat, and simmer for 5 minutes
3. Remove from heat and steep for 10 minutes
4. Strain through a fine mesh strainer and refrigerate

---

## Steps

1. Combine gin, elderflower liqueur, lavender honey syrup, egg white, and orange bitters in a shaker **without ice**
2. Dry shake vigorously for 15 seconds to emulsify the egg white
3. Add ice and shake again until well chilled
4. Double-strain into a chilled coupe
5. The egg white foam will settle on top naturally

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings (without egg white — add per glass):*

- 16 oz gin
- 4 oz elderflower liqueur
- 6 oz lavender honey syrup
- 16 dashes orange bitters

Combine and refrigerate. Dry shake each serving individually with 1 egg white before adding ice.

---

## Notes

The dry shake is essential — it builds the foam before ice dilutes the mixture. A London Dry gin lets the lavender and elderflower shine; a more botanical gin like Hendricks also works beautifully. If avoiding raw egg, aquafaba (chickpea liquid) is a solid substitute.
