## Ingredients

- 2 oz bacon-washed bourbon
- 1 bar spoon maple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- Orange peel or bacon strip, for garnish

---

## House-Made Bacon-Washed Bourbon

*Makes one bottle. Keeps indefinitely refrigerated.*

1. Bake a tray of bacon until crispy — 15 minutes at 375°F
2. Save the rendered grease in a jar and let it cool, but not harden
3. Add bourbon to the jar and let it sit for 4+ hours at room temperature
4. Transfer to the freezer for 1+ hours until the fat solidifies on top
5. Strain through a coffee filter into a fresh jar to remove all solids

---

## Steps

1. Combine fat-washed bourbon, maple syrup, and bitters in a mixing glass with ice
2. Stir slowly until well chilled and diluted — about 45 seconds
3. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube
4. Garnish with an orange peel or a strip of the bacon you cooked

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings:*

- 16 oz bacon-washed bourbon
- 8 bar spoons maple syrup
- 16 dashes Angostura bitters

Stir with ice until diluted, strain into a bottle, refrigerate. Pour over large ice cube to serve.

---

## Notes

Fat-washing is a technique that infuses the fat's flavor into the spirit, then removes the fat through freezing. The result is a silky, bacon-scented bourbon with no grease. Use a bold bourbon — something that can stand up to the fat and maple. Knob Creek or Elijah Craig work well.
