# Prosecco Mojito

> *A bubbly, festive twist on the mojito — lighter on rum, topped with prosecco.*

**Glass:** Highball or wine glass
**Method:** Muddled, topped
**Ice:** Crushed or cubed
**Difficulty:** Easy

---

## Ingredients

- 1½ oz white rum
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice
- ½ oz simple syrup
- 8–10 fresh mint leaves
- 2 oz prosecco
- Mint sprig, for garnish

---

## Steps

1. Add mint leaves and simple syrup to a glass and gently muddle — press, don't shred
2. Fill glass with ice
3. Add rum and lime juice, stir briefly
4. Top with prosecco and stir once gently
5. Garnish with a mint sprig

---

## How to Batch It

*Makes 8 servings:*

- 12 oz white rum
- 6 oz fresh lime juice
- 4 oz simple syrup
- Large handful of mint, muddled in the batch and strained out

Combine rum, lime, syrup, and muddled mint. Refrigerate. Add 2 oz prosecco per glass at serve time.

---

## Notes

Don't over-muddle the mint — you want the oils, not the bitterness from the stems. Prosecco adds effervescence without the sweetness of tonic. Cava or any dry sparkling wine works just as well.
