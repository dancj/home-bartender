---
title: Peach Syrup
blurb: "Fresh peach syrup made by maceration instead of boiling."
ingredients:
  - 1 ripe peach
  - ¾ cup sugar
  - ¾ cup water
steps:
  - Chop the peach into cubes and put them in a bowl.
  - Add the sugar, stir to coat, and refrigerate covered overnight, until the fruit releases its juice and it turns syrupy.
  - Add the water, stir to dissolve any remaining sugar, and strain.
hero_image: ./peach-syrup.jpg
---

## Notes

Maceration keeps the fresh peach flavor better than boiling the peaches.
