---
title: "Spiced Syrup"
blurb: "Rich simple steeped with cinnamon, star anise, clove, and allspice — one bottle that fall-ifies an Old Fashioned, a sour, or a margarita."
yield: "Makes ~1½ cups. Keeps 2–3 weeks refrigerated."
ingredients:
  - "2 cups white sugar"
  - "1 cup water"
  - "3 cinnamon sticks"
  - "3 star anise"
  - "6 whole cloves"
  - "1 tsp whole allspice berries"
steps:
  - "Combine the sugar and water in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves."
  - "Add the spices and simmer gently for 5 minutes."
  - "Take off the heat and steep 30 minutes."
  - "Strain, cool, then bottle and label."
---

## Notes

Use it in place of simple or demerara: a barspoon in an Old Fashioned, ¾ oz in a Whiskey Sour, or ¼ oz in the Spiced Cider Highball.
