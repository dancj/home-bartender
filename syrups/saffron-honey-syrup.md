---
title: Saffron Honey Syrup
blurb: "Honey syrup infused with saffron threads."
ingredients:
  - 1 part honey
  - 1 part water
  - A pinch or two of saffron threads
steps:
  - Bring the honey and water to a boil with the saffron threads.
  - Pour into a jar and let it infuse in the fridge.
  - Strain out the threads before use.
---

## Notes

Infusion time is still being worked out.

Saffron is expensive: a small bottle ran about $25 at a local grocery store, and an organic food shop had it for a bit less. A pinch or two per batch goes a long way.
