---
title: Spiced Cranberry Syrup
blurb: "Cranberry syrup simmered with orange, cinnamon, clove, star anise, and nutmeg."
yield: Keeps refrigerated for up to 2 weeks.
ingredients:
  - 1 cup fresh or frozen cranberries
  - 1 cup unsweetened cranberry juice
  - 1 cup granulated white sugar
  - Peels from 1 orange
  - Juice from ½ orange
  - 2 cinnamon sticks
  - 3–4 whole cloves
  - 1 star anise
  - ¼ tsp ground nutmeg
  - 1 tbsp Grand Marnier (optional)
steps:
  - In a medium saucepan, combine all ingredients.
  - Place over low heat, stirring occasionally until the sugar has completely dissolved.
  - Gently simmer over low heat for 15–20 minutes, until the cranberries begin to burst.
  - Remove from the heat and steep for another 10–15 minutes.
  - Strain through a fine-mesh strainer, gently pressing the cranberries to release their juices.
  - Cool completely, then bottle and refrigerate.
---

## Notes

Keep the heat low so the syrup doesn't reduce and thicken. For a plain cranberry syrup, leave out the spices and Grand Marnier but keep the orange peel and juice. Source: [Join Jules](https://joinjules.com/hosting/spiced-cranberry-syrup/).
