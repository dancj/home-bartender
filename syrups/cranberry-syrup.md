---
title: Cranberry Syrup
blurb: "Tart, bright cranberry syrup for the Cranberry Mule, margarita and bourbon sour."
yield: Makes about 1½ cups. Keeps refrigerated for about 2 weeks.
ingredients:
  - 340 g fresh cranberries
  - 1 cup sugar
  - 1 cup water
  - 1 wide strip of orange peel, no white pith (optional)
  - 1 cinnamon stick (optional)
steps:
  - Rinse the cranberries and pick out any soft or shriveled berries.
  - In a small pot, bring the water and sugar to a simmer over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  - Add the cranberries and simmer gently until most of them have burst and the liquid turns deep red.
  - Take the pot off the heat and mash lightly with a spoon. Drop in the orange peel and cinnamon stick, if using, and let it steep.
  - Pour through a fine strainer and press gently with the back of a spoon. Don't force the pulp through, or the syrup turns cloudy and thick.
  - Let it cool, then bottle and label it.
---

## Notes

The leftover pulp makes a quick cranberry sauce or toast topping.

To test it: 2 oz bourbon, ½ oz lime, ½ oz cranberry syrup, top with ginger beer over ice. Taste, then adjust the syrup up or down a barspoon.
