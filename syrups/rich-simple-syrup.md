---
title: "Rich Simple Syrup"
blurb: "2:1 simple syrup — sweeter and silkier than 1:1, so you pour less."
yield: "Makes ~1½ cups. Keeps about a month refrigerated."
ingredients:
  - "2 cups white sugar"
  - "1 cup water"
steps:
  - "Combine in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until the sugar fully dissolves. Don't boil."
  - "Cool, then bottle and label."
