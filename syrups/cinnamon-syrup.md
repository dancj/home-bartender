---
title: Cinnamon Syrup
blurb: "Simple syrup simmered and steeped with cinnamon sticks."
yield: Keeps refrigerated for 2–3 weeks.
ingredients:
  - 1 cup water
  - 1 cup white sugar
  - 3–4 cinnamon sticks
steps:
  - Combine in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  - Simmer 10 minutes.
  - Remove from heat, let steep 30 minutes, then strain.
---
